Keep the promises you put off with one person who notices.

Nodtry is a mutual 1:1 accountability app where you and one partner plan small commitments, take action, check in, encourage each other, and keep going.

Solo intentions can quietly disappear when you want to study, stay active, or rebuild a daily routine. In Nodtry, you connect by mutual choice with one accountability partner who notices your effort, while you do the same for them. It is built for support and respect, not surveillance.

Use Nodtry to

- Keep up a study routine or exam preparation
- Build a walking, workout, or stretching habit
- Continue daily routines such as reading, tidying, or drinking water
- Turn a private intention into a shared commitment with one person

Plan → act → check in and encourage → continue

- Connect with one person
Use an invite code to connect with one accountability partner. Both people take part in a mutual 1:1 relationship and can see each other's commitments.

- Make a small plan
Choose a practical action for study, exercise, or daily life, then set the days, frequency, and reminder time.

- Take action today
See today's commitments in the Now tab and record them as done, rested, or skipped. You can also use the focus timer to start right away.

- Check in and encourage each other
Confirm your partner's action and send a reaction or short message. When a commitment is missed, a knock-knock reminder can offer a gentle reason to begin again.

- Review and continue
Look back on actions and partner check-ins in History. If both of you want, you can also agree on rewards and penalties in advance.

Nodtry is not another place to pile up tasks. It gives your small commitments one person who notices, so they do not quietly disappear.
